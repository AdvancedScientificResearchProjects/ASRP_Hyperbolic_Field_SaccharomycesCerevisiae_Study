## Verdict: matches

The plan's second sentence is an equivalence test in this paper's sense, and the wording on the page is the correct plain reading of it. The paper asks for the bound to be justified by the smallest effect worth caring about; the draft borrows 0.80-1.25 from drug bioequivalence practice and marks it for a statistician. Small arithmetic point: 0.80 is a fall of a fifth and 1.25 a rise of a quarter, so "a quarter or more" is exact only upward.
