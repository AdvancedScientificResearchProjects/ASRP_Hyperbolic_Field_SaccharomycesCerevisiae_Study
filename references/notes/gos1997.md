## What the source says

- "The data from several independent series of exposure experiments and from control experiments show no consistently significant differences [between] exposed and unexposed cells." (Abstract)
- "This is in contrast to previous studies claiming nonthermal effects of electromagnetic fields in this frequency range on the division of S. cerevisiae cells." (Abstract)
- "Two identical test chambers were constructed in one exposure system to perform concurrent control and test experiments" (Abstract)

## Sample and design

Exponentially growing yeast, 41.682-41.710 GHz in 2 MHz steps at 0.5 and 50 µW/cm², two identical chambers run at the same time, division scored by time-lapse photography. Numbers of runs: NOT FOUND (no full text).

## Verdict: n/a (context, no ASRP claim)

A reported field effect on yeast growth (Grundler and Keilmann 1983) was repeated with concurrent controls and did not hold. The design answer was the same as in the campaign plan: an identical unexposed chamber at the same time and place. The frequency range and device are unrelated to the ASRP emitter, so this is a precedent for the method, not evidence about the emitter.
