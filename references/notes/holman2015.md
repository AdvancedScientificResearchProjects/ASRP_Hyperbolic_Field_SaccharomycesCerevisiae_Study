## Verdict: matches

The source supports the campaign's reasoning: knowing the group tends to enlarge the effect. Scope: the pairs come from evolutionary biology and many outcomes there are judged by eye; the paper does not study automatic cell counts. In the pilot the route for bias is the taking of the photo (which field, how sharp), not the count.
