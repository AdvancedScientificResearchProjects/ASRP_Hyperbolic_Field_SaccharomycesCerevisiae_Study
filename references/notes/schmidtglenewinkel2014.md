## Verdict: overstated

The paper does show division and volume growth coming apart, but only in genetically rewired cells, and the result is a breakdown: the cells shrink or swell from division to division and then stop dividing. In wild-type cells the two stay coordinated. So the paper does not make "faster division with the same biomass" a stable state an outside influence could produce in ordinary baker's yeast. Separately, "faster division, same count" is not coherent on its own terms: faster division gives more cells.
