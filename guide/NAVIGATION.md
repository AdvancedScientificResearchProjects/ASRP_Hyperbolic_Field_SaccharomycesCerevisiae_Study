## What each folder is for

- `data/` is observation: the photographs and their journal mapping. The analysis bins in `control-NN` and `sample-chXX` are empty placeholders awaiting attribution.
- `results/` is output of the scripts: counts, occupancy, morphometry and LLM scoring. Everything in `reports/` is derived from it.
- `scripts/` holds the cyto2 and occupancy computer vision and the LLM scoring scripts. The tools listed in the planned stack are not here.
- `reports/` holds the protocol and one report.
- `tools/` and `tests/` belong to this showcase: facts, diagrams, excerpts, a no-loss check against the archived README.

## How the pages are built

Numbers on these pages are computed by `tools/facts.py` and written in by `tools/build_facts.py` (invisible markers). Diagrams come from `tools/build_visuals.py`. Front-page excerpts are pulled from the detail pages by `tools/build_readme_excerpts.py`, so a front-page sentence and its detail page cannot drift. `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests` rebuilds everything and fails on any difference or on any loss against the archived README (`python3 tools/verify_no_loss.py`).
